# PART 1 · VISUAL DNA

### Brand DNA
Saptham is **The Maestro** — a trained Bharatanatyam dancer who is also an engineer; heritage mastered so completely it can bend toward the new. *"Where Tradition Meets Technology."* Reverent, disciplined, authentic, emotional, continuous, innovative, warm. It bows before it performs. It is never loud; when it performs, the room goes silent.

### Visual DNA
**Darkness is the stage; gold is light, not decoration; space is silence.** A candlelit temple-museum where each object is lit like a Cartier jewel. Warm near-black grounds (`#0E0B08`); antique temple-brass gold as *lit aged metal*; jewel-toned silk accents drawn from the seven swaras. Calligraphic curves in tension with a machined grid. High contrast, deep shadow, precious highlights, one soloist per frame. Restraint over ornament — one perfect gold line beats a hundred.

### Emotional DNA
The **Navarasa** in service of one arc: **awe → curiosity → wonder → pride → immersion → tenderness → exhilaration → peace/resonance.** The visitor should *feel they attended a recital*, not browsed a site. The final, lingering emotion is **resonance** — the hum in the air after the last note.

### Motion DNA
**Motion is music.** Nothing snaps; everything has attack, sustain, and decay like a struck veena string. The site keeps time to a **tala** and drones on an unbroken **tambura sruti**. Scroll = time = the unfolding raga. Slow, breath-eased entrances; the whole experience opens *unmetered* (aalap), builds to a climax (Varnam), and resolves (Mangalam). Self-drawing kolam, light that kindles, gold that ignites.

### One-line visual language
> **A candlelit Carnatic recital staged inside a night-museum — warm black and temple gold, one jewel of light at a time, breathing to a tambura drone, rendered with Apple precision and Aesop restraint, and choreographed to unfold across scroll like a Bharatanatyam margam through one octave of the seven swaras.**

# PART 8 · ASSET STYLE REFERENCES (exact artistic language per category)

| Category | Artistic language |
|---|---|
| **Musical Instruments** | Two modes: (a) flat gold calligraphic **line** (1.5px, yali-curl terminals) for icons/emblems; (b) photoreal **lit aged brass** renders (vitrine, single warm key). Never realistic-cluttered clip-art. Tambura = the ambient drone (background/gradient), not an icon |
| **Temple** | Gopuram/mandapam/arch as **structure & wayfinding** — silhouettes, gold-line frames, or weathered-stone environments. Vertical tiering = hierarchy. Never a tourist photo or cartoon temple |
| **Lotus** | Ashtadala 8-petal, golden-ratio gold line; or a single rim-lit bloom on dark water. The "opening/reveal" motif (Alapadma). Never a flat clip-art lotus |
| **Kolam** | Continuous unbroken gold line around a dot grid; self-drawing. THE signature device — dividers, loaders, generative transitions. Never broken or filled |
| **Rangoli** | The ONE place controlled jewel color leads — radial, symmetric, gold-outlined, one jewel per piece. Ceremonial only, used as a jewel. Never rainbow/calendar-art |
| **Decorations (borders/ornaments)** | Fine gold line — bhairavi bands, paisley, yali, hamsa, corner flourishes. Punctuation, not paragraphs. Never busy or Victorian |
| **Dust** | Soft self-luminous warm-gold motes, low density, "made of air." Never hard specks or snow-storm density |
| **Particles** | Incense smoke, ash, jasmine petals, embers — soft-edged, warm, sparse, isolated on alpha. Never neon or hard graphics |
| **Architecture** | Chiaroscuro environments, deep stage darkness, volumetric warm light, layer-separable for parallax. Never flat/cold/cluttered |
| **Icons** | Brass line, 1.5px, calligraphic terminals, ashtadala grid, mudra/instrument metaphors. Never modern-corporate/emoji/duotone |
| **Typography** | High-contrast display serif (Fraunces/Canela) in gold/ivory + machined grotesque + mono; Tamil (Noto Serif Tamil) at equal dignity. Tradition×Technology made literal. Never a single generic sans |
| **Buttons** | Brass-line with gold-ignite hover (light fills L→R); kolam-draw underlines. Never glossy plastic or flat material-design |
| **Cards** | Arch/brass-line frames, swara-tinted accents, marble/silk fills, generous negative space. Never flat drop-shadow web cards |
| **Illustrations** | "The Living Line" — gold calligraphic line on dark, self-drawing, optional gold-leaf fill; mural/kolam lineage. Never flat corporate-Memphis or 3D-blob |

# PART 9 · NEGATIVE STYLE RULES (what must NEVER appear)

**Absolute prohibitions — reject on sight:**
- ❌ Neon, glow-cyberpunk, synthwave, holographic
- ❌ Cold blue/white light, daylight-flat, LED clinical
- ❌ Glossy plastic, chrome, flat-yellow "gold", shiny new metal
- ❌ Random/rainbow gradients, oversaturation, HDR glare
- ❌ Modern corporate/flat/material-design icons, emoji, duotone
- ❌ Stock-photo aesthetics (smiling groups at camera, plastic retouch)
- ❌ Cartoon proportions, mascots, chibi, clip-art gods/deity caricature
- ❌ Inconsistent lighting (mixed directions/temperatures in one frame)
- ❌ On-camera flash, blown highlights, harsh event-photography look
- ❌ Clutter, busy stages, banners, plastic chairs, wires
- ❌ Glassmorphism/frosted-glass UI, blurry web gradients
- ❌ Watermarks, signatures, gibberish text, UI chrome baked into art
- ❌ Wide-angle face distortion; gratuitous Dutch tilts
- ❌ Bollywood-poster glamour; government-portal/matrimonial calendar aesthetic
- ❌ Western Christmas-gold / champagne-gold (must be *temple* brass)
- ❌ Verdigris-green bronze (patina stays warm)
