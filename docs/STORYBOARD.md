# SAPTHAM — THE SCROLL NARRATIVE
### Cinematic storyboard & experience design · v1.0
**Read with `DESIGN_BIBLE.md` and `CANON.md`. This is the narrative spine every scene, asset, and animation serves. It designs the website as one continuous cinematic journey — a museum walk crossed with a live Carnatic recital.**

---

## THE BIG IDEA — "ONE OCTAVE, ONE RECITAL"

The entire scroll is **a single Bharatanatyam Margam performed as a climb through one octave (saptak)**. The visitor descends into a dark sabha, a lamp is lit, and the seven notes rise — **Sa → Ri → Ga → Ma → Pa** builds to the Varnam climax (Events), then **Da → Ni** are the reflective, memory-soaked descent (Gallery, Alumni), before the melody **resolves home to Sa** at the blessing (Footer). Scroll = time = the unfolding raga. The visitor is the conductor; the tambura drones beneath everything as the unbroken sruti.

**Two felt registers held at once:** the *museum* (curation, spotlighting, reverent silence, one precious object at a time) and the *concert* (rhythm, breath, build, release). Never a "webpage." Always a *movement*.

**Route note:** the Home scroll carries Scenes 00–05 + 14 as one unbroken vertical performance; Events/Workshops/Competitions/Gallery/Contact (Scenes 06–13) are deep-dive movements reached by **light-wipe route transitions** that feel like the house lights dimming for the next piece — never a hard page cut.

**Swara accent law:** each scene owns exactly ONE swara hue (per `CANON.md` §2). The octave arc governs the color journey; the black + gold base is constant.
